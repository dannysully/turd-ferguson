Your path through the framework

Tiers 1 and 2, in the right order — built on the authority you already have.

The order of the framework is fixed; the number of tiers is not. Because your authority foundation already exists (DR 62), this engagement goes straight into Tiers 1 and 2 and runs strategic link building from day one — no link-gap analysis or brand-mention groundwork needed.

1

Compounding foundation — SEO + AEO In scope

Build ~16 dedicated child pages under your service subfolders (e.g. /services/design/web-design-for-architects), each engineered around one near-zero-difficulty cluster. Fix the technical base, and engineer AI Overview / LLM citations on top of the entity work. These are the pages the whole link and content programme points at.

Scope, cadence & commercials

A 3-month engagement: build the foundation, build authority to it, convert.

What's included

  • →~16 dedicated subfolder pages — specced by Naz, built by the Series Eight dev team, each structured around its cluster with FAQ / AEO blocks and schema baked in.
  • →Technical SEO — full audit and remediation plan (crawlability, load speed, indexation, entity architecture + Schema.org).
  • →AEO — AI Overview / LLM citation targeting and visibility tracking across a defined prompt set.
  • →Tracking & baseline — analytics and Search Console repaired; Microsoft Clarity stood up for heatmaps and session recordings.
  • →Strategic link building — alwayscited “best of” and contextual placements pointed at the live pages with deliberate anchors.
  • →Internal linking & content — every relevant case study links to its dedicated page; vertical content sequenced by cluster priority; CRO from end of Month 2.

Team

Naz — project lead · technical SEO (spec) · AEO · content · internal linking · link-building map
Matt — tracking & analytics audit · baseline · Microsoft Clarity
Kev — strategic link building (alwayscited placements)

Commercials

A 3-month term, funded by spreading the Vibe Retail referral fee owed to Series Eight across the engagement — converting an outstanding payable into delivered growth work on your own asset.

The three months

Month 1 — Build the foundation

The build month. Dedicated pages specced and shipped via the dev team; technical + tracking audit; Clarity live; pages internally linked from their hero case studies; keyword-to-page link map produced.

Month 2 — Build links to the pages

With pages live and indexed, Kev's strategic links point at them; technical remediation implemented; AEO / schema live; content expanded; Clarity reviewed and CRO drafted.

Month 3 — Convert & prove

Link building continues; internal linking loop completed; AEO citations tracked against baseline; first CRO tests live; end-of-term review of rankings, qualified GB/US traffic and AI citation growth.

What happens next

At kickoff we agree dev-team turnaround expectations (the Month 1 build is the critical path), secure Naz direct CMS access, establish the tracking baseline and stand up Clarity, and lock the cluster build order — Architecture and Wellness Tech first.
